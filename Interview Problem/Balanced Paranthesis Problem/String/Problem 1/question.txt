Problem Statement

Input: A string of comma separated numbers. The numbers 5 and 8 are always present in the list, and it is assumed that 8 always comes after 5.

You have to perform the following operations:

Case 1:
num1 = Sum of all numbers which do not lie between 5 and 8 in the input list.

Case 2:
num2 = A number formed by concatenating all the numbers from 5 to 8 (including 5 and 8).

Output:
Print the sum of num1 and num2.

Example

Input:
3,2,6,5,1,4,8,9

Explanation

Numbers between 5 and 8 are:
1,4

These numbers are ignored for num1.

Remaining numbers:
3,2,6,9

num1 = 3 + 2 + 6 + 9 = 20

Numbers from 5 to 8:
5,1,4,8

Concatenation:
num2 = 5148

Final Output:

num1 + num2 =
20 + 5148 = 5168
